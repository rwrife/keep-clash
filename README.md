# Keep Clash

Offline 3D castle-shield ball battle: deflect fireballs, smash rival keeps, and win couch or solo rounds on Windows, tablets, and phones.

**Status:** Godot 4.7.2-stable. Playable solo-vs-bots/local-seat rounds, pause/restart/rematch, per-device seat mapping and Windows/Android-aware back/quit handling. #4 PBR primitives, impact effects and imported generated orb are implemented; #15 adds an original modeled shield with three GLB LODs; pinned Linux Godot import and GUT pass, but rendered/device checks remain pending. #14 adds an original modeled keep with three imported GLB LODs; pinned Linux Godot import, GUT and 10-round smoke pass. Rendered/device checks remain pending. Three arena variants, star unlocks, cosmetic palettes and versioned local save/settings are implemented for #5, subject to its #4 dependency. See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and [issues](https://github.com/rwrife/keep-clash/issues).

## Why / for whom

For friends wanting short couch competitions, and solo players wanting an arcade duel without accounts, ads, or paid upgrades. Inspired by a general corner-defense arcade mechanic; all names, rules, art, sound, geometry, and levels are original. No Atari assets, trademarks, or copied boards.

## Intended play

Choose solo versus three configurable bot shields or local pass-and-play/paired-controller mode; steer a shield on an arc around your keep and redirect a shared energy orb through rivals' destructible ramparts. Last keep standing wins. Earn local stars for clean deflections and survival, unlock arena variants and cosmetics, then retry with changed bot difficulty. Mouse/keyboard/gamepad on Windows; touch drag and on-screen buttons on phones/tablets. No multiplayer server. Four-human input needs distinct devices/controllers; touch is one local player in MVP.

MVP: deterministic ball/shield collision rules, four keeps and destructible wall segments, AI opponents, short rounds, local scores/progression, three arenas, pause/settings (audio/remap/accessibility), save/restore, adaptive HUD, impact audio/particles/camera feedback. Non-goals: copied classic levels, online matchmaking, user accounts, purchases, social features, physics-perfect retro emulation, or medical/safety claims. Engine non-goals, explicit: no Unity, no Flutter, no React Native, no Expo, no Kotlin Multiplatform, no .NET MAUI, no custom or browser-only engine — Godot 4 only.

## Privacy / permissions / storage

Local versioned JSON under Godot user data contains all settings (including controls), stars, unlocked arena/cosmetic selections, and records. Unlocks are derived from the exported star total. No accounts, telemetry, network access, or broad storage permissions are required; Android network permissions are explicitly disabled and checked by the CI GUT suite.

Use **EXPORT BACKUP** / **IMPORT BACKUP (REPLACE PROFILE)** on the menu to choose a local JSON file through the platform's native save/open dialog. Export copies the active profile without changing the game save; keep backups somewhere private (a user-chosen synchronized folder may be synced by its provider, not by the game). Import replaces the entire profile, applies settings live, and accepts validated schema v2 or supported v1 migration. Empty, oversized, malformed, or unsupported backups leave the current save byte-for-byte intact and leave active settings/progress unchanged. Writes stage and verify a sibling temporary file before atomic replacement; imports also recheck the loaded save for external changes immediately before replacement. Invalid/unknown existing saves remain read-only, including for import, until addressed outside the game.

Canceling a dialog changes nothing. Platforms without native file-dialog support report that limitation rather than using an in-game picker or requesting permissions. Deleting app data clears local progress, but not exported backups. This partial #7 change has headless tests only; native mobile file-dialog support and device behavior remain unverified. #5 remains open as a dependency, so #7 stays open.

## Play this build

The round camera fits the entire rail-bounded arena and keep heights into the space between the HUD and bottom controls, including when resizing or using portrait displays.

**Power bump:** tap **Space** (keyboard), **right mouse** (mouse seat), **A / bottom face button** (gamepad seat), or that seat's on-screen **BUMP** button just before the orb contacts your shield. The timing window is 0.18 seconds, with a 1.2-second cooldown even on a miss. A successful bump more than doubles the orb's normal speed for 3 seconds (capped at 52 m/s); subsequent boosts refresh rather than stack. Normal acceleration resumes after the boost expires. Bump controls are fixed; steering and pause remain remappable.

**Arena targets:** hit the labeled, colored orbs with a ball to activate them. **SPLIT** creates up to two extra balls (maximum three in play) for 12 seconds; **SPEED** boosts the hitting ball for 3 seconds; **SHRINK** narrows every surviving shield to 55% of its usual arc for 8 seconds, without changing its distance from the keep. Targets return 10 seconds after being hit. Effects and bump cooldowns freeze while paused and reset on restart/rematch. Extra balls can break walls and eliminate keeps; a core hit removes only that ball while others remain, and the final remaining ball continues play until the next serve or round end. The HUD shows bump readiness and active effect timers.

Install pinned Godot 4.7.2-stable and run `godot --path .` (or open project in editor). Choose **SOLO ROUND** for bottom keyboard seat versus three bots; cycle bot difficulty on menu. **SEAT MAPPING / COUCH PLAY** lets you claim distinct keyboard, mouse, touch or connected gamepads for any of four seats (D-pad/shoulder press claims a gamepad); unclaimed seats fill with bots. Keyboard A/D or ←/→ rotates your shield; mouse aims toward arena point near claimed keep; gamepad left-stick X rotates; touch drag rotates. Esc/P pauses or resumes; pause menu offers restart/menu; round-over offers rematch/menu. Stars and records save to versioned `user://keep_clash.json`; arena variants unlock at 3 and 8 stars, shield/keep cosmetics at 3 and 6. Settings menu adjusts audio, control bindings, HUD scale, contrast and motion live; changes persist across restarts. Device mapping is local; real phone/controller inputs remain to be checked on hardware. Run `godot --headless -s tools/smoke_ten_rounds.gd` for the 10-round headless smoke and the GUT command above for tests. With arena targets enabled, the bot-only smoke includes both time-limit and knockout finishes; balance remains open.
